The sequence

Only the final step is optional. The receiving cell may change voltage without firing.

Step 1 Incoming nerve spike

A brief electrical spike travels along the first neuron’s axon.

Step 2 Transmitter release

The spike reaches the terminal and causes chemical transmitter to be released.

Step 3 Synaptic current

The transmitter opens channels. Ions flow across the receiving cell’s membrane.

Step 4 Voltage response

The current changes the receiving neuron’s voltage over several milliseconds.

Step 5 - maybe New nerve spike

This happens only if the combined voltage reaches the cell’s firing threshold.
