Comparison method

All models receive the same input spikes.

Scoring gives equal weight to magnitude and waveform shape, measured with relative RMSE and cosine similarity.

The graph’s 220 ms window moves through the full train. Scores use complete responses from all five prediction protocols, excluding calibration inputs.
