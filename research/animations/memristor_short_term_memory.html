How can a memristor hold short-term memory?

A voltage pulse changes the device state. The state then relaxes towards its baseline. A later pulse can arrive before this change disappears, so the device responds with recent-pulse history.

What creates the short-term memory?

  • 1. Write: A pulse raises the temporary state.
  • 2. Retain: The state relaxes with time.
  • 3. Read: Conductance reports the remaining state.
