Sequence

Step 1 Presynaptic voltage

The input neuron sends a voltage waveform to the sampling electrode.

Step 2 Voltage across the device

The interface applies the sampled voltage across the memristor.

Step 3 Conductance increases

Voltage increases the model’s internal state and conductance.

Step 4 Postsynaptic current

Conductance and neuron voltage set the injected current. Current continues between spikes.

Step 5 Receiving neuron may spike

The receiving neuron fires when its voltage reaches −36 mV.
